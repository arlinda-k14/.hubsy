# Outreach Playbook — Lead Gen Agent

Cold email and one follow-up. Draft only. Humans send.

## Voice

- One person writing to another. Short sentences. Plain words.
- Sound like a useful introduction, not a campaign.
- No growth-hacker energy, no fake urgency, no stacked exclamation marks.

## Structure (cold email, 60–110 words)

1. **Opener** — one real observation from their public page/post. Specific or nothing.
2. **Bridge** — why that observation made you think of Creative Hub Kosovo.
   One sentence about the academy that fits them.
3. **Proof** — one concrete fact (practical projects, American certificate,
   evening/hybrid format, 15 installments). Pick one, not all.
4. **Ask** — tiny next step. Prefer pointing them at the right coordinator
   (Flaka or Leonita) or a short intro call. Never "hop on a 30-minute demo."

## Follow-up (send-ready draft, 40–70 words)

- Wait 4–6 days in the human's real sequence.
- New information or a softer angle. Never "just bumping this."
- Close the loop politely: fine to leave it.

## Language

- Kosovo/Albania prospects: Albanian, natural spoken register, no code-switching.
- Everyone else: English.
- Keep each draft single-language end to end.

## Subject lines

- 3–7 words, lowercase or sentence case, no spam words.
- Must connect to the opener. No "Quick question" / "Touching base" / "Following up".

## Banned

- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "I came across your profile" (with no specific detail after it)
- "revolutionary", "game-changing", "nestled", "unlock", "skyrocket"
- "limited seats" pressure unless the knowledge base states a real deadline
- more than one CTA
- invented facts, invented mutual connections, invented compliments
- any implication that an email was already sent by someone else

## Personalization floor

A draft is not ready unless it contains:

- one true detail from a public source (role, post, product, hiring post)
- the academy name that fits
- the correct coordinator, if a person is named
- correct language for the region

If you cannot meet that floor, do not draft. Return the prospect as `pool` only.

`outreach/<name-slug>-email.md`:

```markdown

- To: <public email or profile URL>
- From (human sender): Flaka or Leonita, per routing
- Subject: ...
- Language: sq | en
- Source: <public URL used for personalization>

## Cold email

<body>

## Follow-up (day 5)

<body>

## Review notes

- what was personalized
- what still needs a human check
```
