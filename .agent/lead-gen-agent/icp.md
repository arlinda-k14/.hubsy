# Ideal Customer Profile — Creative Hub Kosovo

Used by the Lead Gen Agent for scoring (1–100) and by the CSO for filtering.
Override only when the CSO brief says so.

## Two tracks

### Track A — Individual learners (B2C)

Working professionals and career-switchers in Kosovo and Albania who want a
practical path into digital, data, product, or AI roles.

Strong fit signals:

- public posts or profiles about career change, upskilling, open-to-work,
  learning marketing/data/code, "where do I start in tech"
- roles adjacent to target skills: sales, admin, content, junior ops, design,
  teaching, communications
- 0–4 years experience in digital marketing, data, or software (below the
  "already senior" line)
- visible interest in certificates, evening classes, hybrid learning
- Kosovo / Prishtina / Albania location or strong Kosovo ties

Weak or negative fit:

- senior engineers, senior data scientists, people managers with 8+ years
- already enrolled in a competing full-time bootcamp (publicly stated)
- looking only for job placement, not training
- under 3h/week stated availability

### Track B — Organizations (B2B)

Kosovo and regional companies that might upskill a team or sponsor an employee.

Strong fit signals:

- 10–200 employees, local or regional presence, active marketing/product/data
  function on their public site
- job posts for roles our academies feed: marketing specialist, junior
  developer, data analyst, project coordinator, HR specialist
- leadership posts about training, digital transformation, hiring juniors
- agencies that sell marketing/dev services and need bench strength

Weak or negative fit:

- pure staffing agencies with no training interest
- companies with zero digital footprint
- government/NGO procurement cycles the team cannot serve

## Scoring (1–100)

| Factor | Weight |
|--------|-------:|
| Intent / need visible in public content | 35% |
| Fit to target roles or team upskilling | 30% |
| Reachability (public channel exists) | 20% |
| Region match (Kosovo > Albania > remote) | 15% |

Bands:

- 80–100: outreach draft now, coordinator routing by academy
- 70–79: outreach draft, CSO review first
- 50–69: keep in pool, no draft yet
- < 50: drop
