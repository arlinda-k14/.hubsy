# Research — data-vibe-week (1-week strategy)

Research date: 2026-10-20. Zero logins. Public pages only.
Queries:
1. `career change into data science 2026 upskilling trends`
2. `vibe coding AI agents learning content trends`
3. Public YouTube search: `career change to data analyst 2026`
4. Public YouTube search: `vibe coding beginners 2026 ai agents`

## Patterns found

Data / analytics content:
- The dominant public angle is market anxiety: "Is a Data Analyst a Dying Career in 2026?",
  "The Brutal TRUTH About the Data Analyst Job Market", "Is it even worth it?"
- Second angle is roadmap/zero-to-job: "How I'd Become a Data Analyst in 2026 (full roadmap)",
  "From Zero to Job-Ready".
- Recurring counter-claim: "Data analysts aren't going away in 2026, but the job is changing."
- Salary and certification content gets clicks; employers opening the portfolio is a
  recurring trust argument.

Vibe coding / AI agents content:
- Volume is high and mostly tutorial-shaped: "How to Vibe Code in 2026 (Full Beginners
  Tutorial)", "Vibe Coding for Beginners (Full Course 2026)".
- The contrarian angle performs: "Vibe Coding is a Trap (What Senior Devs See That You Don't)".
- "AI agents" appears in 5 of 5 retained sources; the term is the hook, debugging and
  shipping are the credibility gap.

## Implications for Creative Hub Kosovo

- Do not add another generic "learn AI" post. The public feed is saturated with that.
- Win by naming the fear (is this career still real?) and answering with proof (portfolio,
  hiring partners, real projects).
- The "trap" angle fits our positioning: anyone can prompt, few can fix and ship.
  That is the Vibe Coding Mastery promise.
- Portfolio-first proof is the strongest differentiator for Data Science & AI.
