## Reference 1 — "The trap" framing

- Public source: YouTube search "vibe coding beginners 2026 ai agents"
  https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=vibe+coding+beginners+2026+ai+agents
- Pattern: contrarian explainers outperform plain tutorials ("Vibe Coding is a Trap").
- Visual idea: split screen. Left, a polished AI demo. Right, the same app with a console
  error. Slow zoom on the error.
- Use for: 2026-10-21 reel (Vibe Coding).

## Reference 2 — Market-anxiety answer

- Public source: YouTube search "career change to data analyst 2026"
  https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=career+change+to+data+analyst+2026
- Pattern: titles open with a fear ("Dying career?", "Brutal truth"), then resolve it with
  a roadmap. Thumbnails use one face + 3–4 words.
- Visual idea: single text-on-screen frame with the fear, cut to a calm talking head
  answering it in one sentence.
- Use for: 2026-10-23 LinkedIn image and 2026-10-25 reel opening.

## Reference 3 — Portfolio-first proof

- Pattern: career content converts when it shows the artifact, not the promise.
- Visual idea: screen-record a scroll through three real student dashboards. No music,

- Use for: 2026-10-25 reel (Data Science).

## Reference 4 — Campus and hybrid credibility

- Public source: https://creativehubkos.com (public site)
- Pattern: local campus footage outperforms stock for regional audiences.
- Visual idea: 3s handheld shot of the Prishtina campus entrance, then a live session
  screen. Caption overlay: "Prishtinë ose online. E njëjta certifikatë."
- Use for: future behind-the-scenes posts; optional cutaway in post 1.

## Reel packaging rules for this week

- First frame: hook text, 6 words max, high contrast, no logo.
- Second 2 seconds: a face or a real screen, never a stock montage.
- Captions burned in; 80% watch muted.
- End card: coordinator photo, name, "20 min" and the Calendly URL as a QR for stories.
- Keep the brand palette neutral; let the screen content carry the colour.
