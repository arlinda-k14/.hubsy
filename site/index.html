Featured platform

Curio

TikTok for smart people — every short-form clip you've made, in one feed that actually works.

Curio is an educational-only short-form video platform: a single place to aggregate, credit and publish vertical video that teaches you something. It filters out the brain-rot and the fluff, so what's left is informative and genuinely useful — reels across curated categories like Marketing, Tech, Data and Coding. Paste a link from YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok or Instagram and it embeds as a native player with creator credit attached; upload an MP4 and it streams straight from the cloud.

  • No brain-rot, by design. Every clip has to inform or teach. Fluff and filler don't make it onto the feed — the filter is the product.
  • Curated categories, front and centre. Marketing, Tech, Data and Coding feeds re-query the library in place, so the right clip is one tap away — never a scroll hunt.
  • Native social embeds. YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok and Instagram links resolve to clean, responsive players with creator credit carried through — no thumbnail dead-ends.
  • A direct MP4 pipeline via Cloudinary. Uploads stream straight from the cloud with low latency, no third-party render queue between upload and playback.
  • Firestore backend. Feeds, categories and creator data run on managed cloud infrastructure, so the platform holds up past the demo stage.

Featured tool

ViralScript AI

Write once. Publish everywhere, in two languages.

Point it at an article, a repository, or a messy wall of notes. It reads the source and produces five structured posts — a hook, the discovery, the mechanism, the application, and the action — sized for X or LinkedIn, in English or Albanian.

  • It refuses to invent numbers. Every figure in a post has to appear in the source. If extraction fails, the run stops and asks for the raw text instead of guessing.
  • Two languages from one source — no translating a finished post and hoping the argument survives.
  • Runs inside your existing assistant — the skills are plain markdown, not a subscription you have to keep paying for.

Now registered inside AIOS as social-thread-writer, so the router can dispatch to it on its own triggers — no special request needed.

New tool

ViralClipAI

One long video in. Three clips you can actually post.

Point it at a YouTube link or a timestamped transcript. It scans for hook moments, emotional beats, and tactical takeaways, then returns the three segments most likely to hold a viewer — each with exact start and end timestamps, a viral score with a reason, a hook overlay for the first three seconds, and a caption with hashtags.

  • It refuses to invent dialogue. Every quote and every timestamp comes from a real cue in the source transcript, and the 15–60s band is enforced before output.
  • Blueprints and rendered files. It writes the timestamped plan, then trims the three segments with ffmpeg into 9:16 clips ready to upload.

AI assistant

Hubsy — AI Conversational Assistant & Lead Advisor

A student advisor that never sleeps, never guesses, and knows when to hand over to a human.

An intelligent customer-facing chatbot widget for Creative Hub Kosovo that acts as an automated digital student advisor — answering program questions, explaining tuition and schedules, and routing real people to the right coordinator.

  • RAG knowledge base. 123 verified facts indexed across 7 academy curricula, so every number quoted matches the source of truth.
